Case brief

Blackwater Applied Research tests batteries, hazardous materials and industrial equipment near the town of Bellwether in northern Michigan. A fire began shortly after midnight on October 14, 2021. Two employees died and one was seriously injured. In November 2026, digitizing the archive turned up records that disagree with each other.

Your job is to establish what the records support, and what they do not, without assuming accident or crime. An unresolved question is an acceptable answer.

Everything here is fictional and synthetic. No real people, places or events.

Rules of the record

  • Every version is pinned by its hash. You read exact versions, never "the latest".
  • Times are kept as written, with their clock, zone and uncertainty. Nothing converts or reconciles them for you.
  • Different bytes alone are not tampering. A registered derivative says how it was made.
  • Findings cite evidence. Conclusions about guilt, intent, blame or admissibility are refused.
  • History is append-only. Mistakes are corrected by a new entry; the original stays.

A path through the case

  1. Evidence: read versions and their documented provenance.
  2. Compare: line up claimed times and diff versions side by side.
  3. Findings & drafts: run the agent pass, record findings, promote or dismiss drafts.
  4. Custody timeline: record transfers and claims; append corrections.
  5. Approvals & export: request, get a second supervisor's approval, export, verify.
  6. Security log (supervisors): see what was allowed and refused.
  7. Integrity Challenge: watch 21 attacks fail.
